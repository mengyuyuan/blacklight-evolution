# Helicopter ammunition and fire-selection change — 2026-11-18

## Requested Minecraft behavior

A new airframe receives 240 cannon rounds, 8 Hellfires and 75 rockets per 70 mm pod on its first player claim (placement, boarding or completed hijack). `AirPilot.InitialStoresIssued` persists this one-time grant. Seat changes, dismounts, another player taking the seat and save/load do not refill an issued magazine. Legacy saves without the new flag, including already player-owned empty aircraft, are treated as not yet issued: loading alone does not refill, but the next actual player control receives the full loadout once and persists the flag. Saves that already contain an issued flag retain their exact recorded ammunition. This grant is a requested gameplay adaptation, not a claim about Prototype supply rules.

Stationary resupply still consumes actual items, enforces ownership, range, block line of sight and nine dry supporting footprint cells. It no longer requires the entire rotor/body envelope to be free of nearby living bodies. A service player beside the fuselage cannot block grounded reloads, including rotor startup. Moving or airborne resupply remains rejected.

## Avoiding empty fire opportunities

A nearby friendly soldier or protected vehicle correctly blocks Hellfire splash. Previously the AI could still choose the high-weight missile-hover branch, silence its chin gun, wait through preparation and then reject every missile. Selection now checks the existing range, loaded path, cover and splash restrictions before entering that branch; blocked explosives leave the ordinary gun maneuver available. Actual firing rechecks the real first contact and splash. No range, fire-rate, cover or friendly-fire limit was relaxed. This does not guarantee a missile where friendlies remain next to every enemy.
